I began my career in the classroom before spending thirteen years building marketing and customer programs for the ed-tech companies teachers rely on every day: Amplify, MyPaymentsPlus, Remind, ParentSquare, and most recently as Director of Customer Marketing at MagicSchool AI.

I didn’t leave education when I left the classroom. I found a different way to serve it.

Now I help other teachers find their own path out of the classroom and into work that pays what they’re worth. You have more skills than you think, and the corporate world needs what you’ve learned.
